AI SMART Goal Coach

A functional prototype for guided Personal Development Plan (PDP) reflection.

Hi! I'm your AI Coach. What is a broad goal you have for your entrepreneurial journey that we can refine into a SMART goal together?

Methodology & Educational Design

This prototype explores how an AI architecture can facilitate student self-reflection for entrepreneurial SMART goal formulation. Rather than generating finalized goals directly, the AI is constrained by a strict system prompt to act purely as a coach. It is designed to analyze the input and ask targeted, iterative questions to ensure the cognitive load and primary learning process remain squarely with the student.
